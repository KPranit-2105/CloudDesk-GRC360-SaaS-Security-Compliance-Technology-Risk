# CloudDesk Technologies — Security Compliance & GRC Operations Project

> **SIMULATED PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY**
> *This repository represents a simulated, institutional-grade Security Compliance and Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) operating model created for demonstration and portfolio purposes. No real employment or actual official certifications are claimed.*

---

## Executive Overview
CloudDesk Technologies is an Enterprise SaaS platform serving ~800 enterprise customers with 600 employees across engineering, cloud operations, and security. To address demanding customer security inquiries and prepare for formal **SOC 2 Type II (Trust Services Criteria)** and **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** audits, this project establishes a centralized compliance program.

### Program Core Architecture
```text
Compliance Requirement
        ↓
    Control
        ↓
 Control Owner
        ↓
Evidence Requirement
        ↓
Evidence Collection & Validation
        ↓
 Control Testing (Design & Operating Effectiveness)
        ↓
   Finding & Deficiency
        ↓
  Remediation Action Plan
        ↓
    Retest & Closure
        ↓
Executive & Audit Reporting
```

---

## Key Portfolio Highlights
1. **Compliance Frameworks**: 100 mapped compliance requirements across SOC 2 (Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Privacy) and ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls.
2. **Control Library**: 120 operational security controls (`CTRL-001` to `CTRL-120`) with clear ownership matrices across 60+ assignments.
3. **Evidence Repository**: 250 evidence records evaluated under rigorous evidence quality standards (Strong, Adequate, Weak, Insufficient, Missing).
4. **Control Testing**: 100 test records covering design and operating effectiveness with explicit sample sizes and testing methodologies.
5. **Flagship Case Study**: Detailed analysis of **Privileged Access Review Evidence Gap (`CTRL-002`)**, distinguishing control operation from evidence retention gaps.
6. **Risk & Vendor Governance**: 50 security/compliance risks evaluated via formula-driven 5x5 matrix (`Likelihood × Impact`) and 50 third-party vendor risk assessments.
7. **Customer Assurance & Response Library**: 50 customer security questionnaire records with a centralized reusable standard response repository (90% answer reuse rate).
8. **Executive & Domain Dashboards**: Formula-driven Excel workbooks covering Executive Readiness (82%), Control Effectiveness (87.5%), Evidence Completion (92%), and 30 KPIs/KRIs.
9. **Interview Preparation**: 120 interview questions with detailed model answers (30 dedicated to Privileged Access), 10 STAR interview frameworks, and 30s/1m/3m/5m project elevator pitches.
